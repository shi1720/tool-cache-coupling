# Venue assessment, 20 September 2026

This is an editorial fit assessment, not an acceptance prediction or a submission receipt. No venue has received this manuscript at the time of this record.

## Ranked candidates

1. **Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR)**. Strongest immediate fit for a focused theoretical analysis of learning-system behavior. Rolling submissions, official TMLR template, anonymous review and supplements, and an active OpenReview profile. Its acceptance criteria emphasize supported claims and audience interest rather than benchmark leadership or method novelty. The limited scope is not itself a prohibition, but author understanding and responsibility remain essential. TMLR prohibits parallel archival peer-reviewed submission of overlapping work. It allows preprints and explicitly non-archival workshops.
   - https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html
   - https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html
   - https://jmlr.org/tmlr/acceptance-criteria.html
2. **AISTATS 2027**. Relevant statistical-learning and reinforcement-learning audience; a more ambitious target given the result's limited conceptual novelty. Abstract deadline: 29 September 2026 AoE; full paper: 6 October 2026 AoE. Eight main-text pages in the official template, anonymous PDF/supplement, mandatory AI use statement and reproducibility checklist. Reciprocal reviewer nomination or justified exemption is required. Accepted work requires an author to intend to register and present in Montreal. Concurrent conference review is prohibited; TMLR's own policy would also prohibit simultaneous TMLR/AISTATS review. No abstract was registered by this task.
   - https://virtual.aistats.org/Conferences/2027/CallForPapers
3. **Machine Learning (Springer)**. Established journal whose scope includes theoretical learning analysis. Requires a contribution information sheet, editable source, author/funding/conflict statements, and no concurrent review at another journal or conference. The journal states there is no submission/publication charge for the standard route; optional open access carries a charge. A focused contribution must still clearly establish why the insight matters. No account or submission has been created here by this task.
   - https://link.springer.com/journal/10994/submission-guidelines
